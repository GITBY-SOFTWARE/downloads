## New

- Settings has an About tab: what Gitby is, its version, and who makes it.

## Improved

- On the Violet theme, the attention colour (unsaved edits, pending asks) is an orchid that fits the theme instead of amber.

## Platforms

- From this release, builds are published for Windows (x64) and Apple silicon Macs only.
