## New

- Your agents run in **Review**, where every claim they propose waits for you, or **Full access**, where their asks for a free file or one you hold are granted as they arrive. Shift+Tab switches it anywhere, the header shows which is on, Settings › AI agents has the switch, and the choice is saved. **In validation.**
