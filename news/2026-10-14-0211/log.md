## New

- The terminal app in Danish and French. Pick a language on the first page of setup or in Settings › Appearance › Language; it starts in that language even offline.
- Edit your own messages in Chat and Threads: select one and press `e`. Enter saves, Esc puts it back, and everyone sees it marked (edited).
- Terminal editors (vim, nano, Helix and others) open in a window of their own, with a tab per file, instead of taking over Gitby's screen. `o` on a file opens it. On macOS and Linux for now. **In validation.**
- See what a teammate did lately: press Enter on them in Online, or click them. **In validation.**

## Improved

- Settings: summaries and descriptions wrap instead of being cut off.
- The Browser page runs on a newer Chromium.
