## New

- `gitby mcp` lets an AI coding agent work through Gitby as `@you/<agent>`: it proposes claims you approve, asks for takeovers with a reason, leaves notes, chats, and commits and pushes through Gitby. Settings › AI agents installs it for Claude Code, Cursor, Windsurf, Codex and Gemini. **In validation.**
- Windows hold pages as tabs. Drag a tab into another window, or onto a window's edge to make a new one. Settings › Reset layout puts everything back.
- A Terminal page runs a real shell in your clone, and Ctrl+T opens another.
- A Commits page lists your recent commits with their details, and a Preview page collects what your agents ask for.

## Improved

- Chat: write in the chat itself, send messages over several lines, and use Markdown with highlighted code, emoji by `:shortcode:` or from a picker, and a copy button on code blocks.
- ← and → switch a window's pages, and keys move you between windows.
- A setting for a transparent background.

## Fixed

- Popups no longer paint a solid colour over a transparent background.
