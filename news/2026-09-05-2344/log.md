## Improved

- Alt+← and Alt+→ switch the pages of the window you are in, even while a terminal has the keyboard. Ctrl and the arrows still move between windows.
- The About tab shows the Gitby wordmark.

## Fixed

- You can accept (`a`) or deny (`d`) an agent's takeover request from the file tree, not only on Preview.
