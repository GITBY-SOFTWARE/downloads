## New

- A **Threads** page: a titled conversation about one issue or feature, with the files it involves attached. `/claim` claims its files, you react to and copy posts from the keyboard, and new posts arrive as they are written. **In validation.**
- Chat from the keyboard: select a message with the arrow keys, react (`r`), copy it (`y`), or delete your own (`Delete`, it asks first).
- Lend a browser tab to your AI assistant, with your consent for each site. **In validation.**
- Every key can be changed, and `?` lists the keys of whatever you are looking at.

## Improved

- In the Browser page, a link that opens a new tab opens as a tab, keys reach pages with each platform's own key codes, and the browser's helper processes end with it (on a Mac they no longer show in the Dock).

## Fixed

- Moving the mouse across the Files panel no longer makes the terminal app lag.
